Why it's different

Most estimating tools multiply weight by a rate. This one runs the job.

A flat $/lb number can't tell you why one spool costs three times as much as another that weighs the same. (Spoiler: it's the fourteen PWHT thermocouples.) Quotestimate breaks every line into the operations a shop actually performs, and prices each one.

Capabilities

Everything that goes into a real fabrication quote.

Every parameter below feeds the same estimate — nothing is a separate spreadsheet.

Piping & Pressure Equipment

  • ASME B31.3 process piping costing, joint by joint
  • ASME Sec. VIII Div. 1, Pressure Vessel fabrication
  • Design pressure/temperature, corrosion allowance, NACE MR0175 sour service
  • Hydrotest and pneumatic test hours, tracked per line
  • Hardness testing (P-No 4/5 alloys, NACE MR0175) and Charpy impact testing (UG-84 MDMT) — in-house or subcontract

Welding & Heat Treatment

  • Weld process, size, and joint count drive labor and consumables
  • Weld Position — flat, horizontal, vertical, or overhead, each with its own productivity multiplier (overhead genuinely takes longer, not just harder)
  • PWHT — furnace, local induction, or local resistance, with hold/ramp hours and thermocouple setup
  • NDE — RT, UT, MT, PT, PMI, VT, VB — in-house time or subcontract cost, with a coverage % for random/spot examination instead of assuming every joint is tested
  • Preheat/interpass temperature and filler metal classification (AWS A5.x)

Heat Exchangers & Structural

  • TEMA Class B/C/R — tube-to-tubesheet joints, expanded or welded
  • Baffle and tie-rod bundle assembly, priced separately from shell fabrication
  • Expansion joint (bellows) labor premium — thin-wall material costs differently than shell plate
  • AISC structural steel, bolt grade, and moment-connection labor premium
  • Welder ID, heat/lot number, MTR reference, and CRN/National Board tracking

Machining & Precision Work

  • Drilling, boring, and CNC milling — cost keyed to hole count, hole size, and axis count (2/3/4/5-axis)
  • Lathe/turning, priced by diameter and length
  • Tolerance Class — standard, precision, or high-precision, each with its own time multiplier
  • Cutting — plasma, oxyfuel, saw, laser, or waterjet, each with its own speed and machine rate
  • Deep-hole boring for large forgings — bore diameter and depth, priced separately from routine hole boring
  • API 16A / 16AR well-control equipment (BOP bodies, rams, bonnets) — Temperature Class, PSL level, and rated working pressure tracked per line, with PSL driving inspection time
  • Outsourced machining priced as a flat subcontract cost, not forced through the shop rate

Sheet Metal & Finishing

  • Press-brake forming with bend-deduction and K-factor flat-pattern calculation
  • Pipe bending and polishing, priced per operation
  • Coating — standard, epoxy, or marine-grade — and cleaning — degrease, pickle/passivate, or sandblast
  • Galvanizing and dimensional/QC inspection, tracked as their own line items

Shop & Business Tools

  • Material nesting, cut optimization, and drop/leftover inventory
  • Import from Autodesk Inventor, AutoCAD, AutoCAD Plant 3D, Cadworks, and other CAD BOM/Material List exports (CSV/Excel) — keyword-based column auto-detection with malformed-row protection, and remembers a corrected layout so a recurring client format is never mapped twice
  • QuickBooks Online sync, PO generation, and job travelers
  • Business Intelligence dashboard — estimate vs. actual, realized margin, and suggested rate adjustments when a cost category runs consistently high or low across completed jobs

Estimate Classification & Quoting

  • AACE 18R-97 Estimate Class 1–5 — contingency tied to the classification you actually pick, not a flat guess
  • Expected Accuracy Range printed on every quote (e.g. -20%/+30% for a Class 3 budget estimate)
  • Governing Code reference — ASME B31.3 / Sec. VIII Div. 1 / Sec. IX, AWS D1.1, AISC 360, API 650 / 620, CSA W47.1 / G40.21 / S16
  • Set per job, not per shop profile — never a stale classification left over from someone else's quote
