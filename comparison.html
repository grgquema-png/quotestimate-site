Quotestimate vs. other estimating software

Quotestimate compared against 11 other cost & estimate software tools in the same field — piping, pressure vessels, heat exchangers, structural steel, and storage tanks — to show where they differ: what scope they cover, how they price, and how much they cost.

Based on public vendor pages · verified 2026-11-14 Competitor prices gathered 2026-10-18 and labelled as reported Prices not published by the vendor = “quote on request”

The biggest difference: scope

Quotestimate is the only tool on this list that covers all five scopes in one estimate, one engine. Every competitor covers only one or two scopes — so if you are bidding a mixed package (pipe rack + vessel + exchanger + tank), you would need four tools and four reconciliations with them.

Quotestimate is the costing step that follows design. The design office completes the ASME code calculations; Quotestimate prices the fabrication of the finished design — code materials such as SA-516-70N plate and SA-333 Gr. 6 pipe, priced operation by operation, with the PWHT, NDE, and hydrotest requirements of B31.3 and Sec. VIII Div. 1 carried into every line.

03

Difference #2: how they price

This is the second big divide. Every tool on the list uses one of three methods — and only one uses operation-by-operation.

Operation-by-operation · time × rate

QUOTESTIMATE ONLY

Each BOM line is broken down into the operations the shop actually does: cutting, forming, fit-up, welding, machining — each priced separately. Weld process, size and joint count drive labour and consumables.

  • Welder efficiency (arc-on %) factor
  • Joint prep separate from fit-up
  • Purge gas per weld hour
  • Position / diameter / shop-vs-field / material productivity factors

So two spools with the same weight but different joints come out with different prices — up to 3× difference per the comparison.

Weight × rate · $/lb

COMPRESS + COSTER

The closest vessel rival uses this — exactly the method Quotestimate positions against: “Most estimating tools multiply weight by a rate…”

  • Shell — $17.00/lb
  • Shell rolling — $3.00/lb
  • Purchased heads — $1,200/head
  • Nozzles — $100/lb

From the official COSTER demo report. Components are priced, not shop operations.

Item database + labour hours

MEP AND GENERAL TOOLS

QuoteSoft, AutoBid Mechanical, ESTmep, FastPIPE/FastDUCT, ProEst, CostOS — quantity × cost database / assemblies, takeoff from PDF, BIM or GIS.

Strong on content library (manufacturer items, Harrison prices, 100k+ items), but no weld-process, PWHT or NDE costing — they were not built for code fabrication.

Labour standards / parametric tables

STRUCTURAL TOOLS

Tekla PowerFab: Labour Standards tables — hours by operation (Burn, Drill, Punch, Weld) by shape/dimension, plus a material pricing database. STRUMIS: parametric labour + connection costing. FabTrol: per-item material + labour from company-standard tables.

This is not flat $/lb — but it covers structural shapes only, and it is tied to their own ERP.

Each competitor, one by one

What they can do, and what they can do that Quotestimate cannot. That last part matters — it is where you see whether they are complements or true replacements.

Quotestimate

QUOTESTIMATE.COM
PipingVesselsExchangersStructuralTanks
  • NDE — RT, UT, MT, PT, PMI, VT, VB; can be combined per joint, in-house or subcontract, with coverage %
  • PWHT — furnace / local induction / local resistance; heating + hold + cooling stages, thermocouple setup
  • Hydrotest and pneumatic test hours per line; hardness testing (P-No 4/5, NACE MR0175), Charpy impact (UG-84 MDMT), NACE sour service
  • AACE 18R-97 Estimate Class 1–5 with class-tied contingency and a printed accuracy range
  • Structural: erection, decking, anchor bolts, fireproofing, stairs/platforms/railings, detailing as a costed line item

BOM Import Checks (v1.0.8). Material designations are checked as the BOM loads: a grade that does not exist, a gasket made of the wrong stuff, or a weight that is thousands of pounds off gets a warning before it reaches the estimate. The checks flag only — they never block a save. No competitor on this page evidences this check.

Beyond the checklist: nesting/cut optimisation + drop inventory; CAD BOM import (Inventor, AutoCAD, Plant 3D, Cadworks, CSV/Excel); standby Autodesk Platform Services (BIM 360/ACC) connector — requires the customer's own APS credentials; QuickBooks Online sync; PO generation; job travellers; Material Master with vendor/freight/FX price history; labour rates by department/crew; BI dashboard (estimate vs. actual in $ and hours per operation, margin, suggested rate adjustments); Subcontractor Bid Comparison; free steel PPI (FRED/BLS) trend reference; region rate profiles with stale-data warnings; client quote with margin hidden; rework/scrap allowance; revision history with BOM snapshots.

Price
$129/mo billed annually ($1,548/yr) · $159/mo billed monthly — per-seat licences that can link into one Company Group. Each licence is hardware-locked and transferable 2×/year. 30-day free trial. Runs locally, stored locally; if the subscription lapses, no new estimates or engine updates, but exports (PDF/Excel/job traveller) remain available.

PipeFitter / Weld Estimator apps

NOT REALLY COMPETITORS
All five

Pipefitter apps are field layout calculators — offsets, takeouts, cut lengths, flange alignment. Weld estimators (FIRGELLI, Let'sFab, Varyos Weld) compute weld-metal weight/cost for one joint from geometry × deposition rate × efficiency.

They estimate one weld, not one job — so they are not comparable products.

Price
Free to ~$2.99/mo or $19.99/yr (Varyos Weld, “launching soon”).

Where Quotestimate stands against this group

  • Built for small and medium fabrication shops only — not EPC CAPEX. Quotestimate prices shop operations on a bill of materials; the enterprise four price capital projects.
  • No drawing/BIM takeoff. CostOS and InEight win there — Quotestimate starts from the BOM, not from drawings or models.
  • API integration is partial. ERP export via CSV/JSON, QuickBooks Online sync, and a standby Autodesk Platform Services connector — but no public REST API.
  • Cloud backup is partial. Storage is local; there is no full cloud backup/restore.
  • Newness risk is real. Quotestimate is a new product from a single developer — see the vendor-longevity gap in the verdict below.
06

Verdict: where it is stronger, where it is weaker

Where Quotestimate is stronger

  1. Scope breadth. It is the only one that covers all five in one estimate, one engine. A shop bidding a pipe rack + vessel + exchanger + tank package does not need four tools or four reconciliations.
  2. Costing depth. It is the only one that prices PWHT (stages + thermocouples + furnace vs. local), NDE by type with coverage %, hydrotest/pneumatic hours, Charpy/hardness, NACE sour service, welder efficiency, joint prep vs. fit-up, purge gas, and shop-vs-field productivity — the items that make two same-weight spools differ by 3×. The closest vessel rival (COSTER) is $/lb.
  3. Shop-to-site structural. Erection (self or sub), decking, anchor bolts, fireproofing, misc metals, detailing, inspection and site prep in one quote — no structural tool evidences these as priced estimate lines.
  4. Built-in API 650 tank calculator — no separate tank software needed.
  5. Transparency and price. Published $129/$159, per-seat licences that can link into one Company Group, 30-day trial, your data stays local, and exports survive even if the subscription lapses. Almost everything else is quote-on-request or a third-party guess.
  6. Estimate discipline. AACE 18R-97 class-tied contingency, printed accuracy range, estimate-vs-actual BI per operation, subcontractor bid comparison, material price history, stale-rate warnings.

Where Quotestimate is weaker — honest gaps

  1. Production control and inventory — now included. Quotestimate tracks each job from Not Started to In Progress to Done, deducts material usage from Inventory automatically when a job starts, and keeps a running in/out log with live balances per material. What it still does not do: capacity planning by department/shift, shop-floor barcode tracking, or shipping — Tekla PowerFab and STRUMIS still lead there.
  2. No native detailing / BIM authoring. Tekla has Structures; ESTmep has CADmep; STRUMIS/FabTrol import from detailers. Quotestimate imports BOMs and has a standby APS connector, but it does not detail. Detailing in Quotestimate is costed, not performed.
  3. CNC: costing + DXF export — yes. Direct machine control / G-code — no. Quotestimate has CNC costing: CNC Milling with 2/3/4/5-axis rates, CNC Drill, and CNC beam-line drilling/punching — plus Export for Plate Nesting (DXF), Import Nesting Results, and a Nesting Report. What it does not do is control the machine directly or send G-code: the user loads the DXF into CAM/the machine. STRUMIS (StruCNC), ESTmep/CAMduct and Tekla drive machines themselves.
  4. Multi-user — now included (Company Multi User). Licences link into one Company Group: see who is online, edit different lines at the same time, and the Master controls rates and finalisation. Each person still has their own paid licence. What it still is not: a central multi-user database with capacity planning like Tekla/STRUMIS — one company only, and estimates still live on each machine.
  5. No code design. COMPRESS / PV Elite (Hexagon) design vessels and exchangers to code; Quotestimate costs a design made by others.
  6. Vendor longevity. New launch, small vendor — its own site FAQ says an installed copy on an unrenewed licence does not have the “same long-term backing as buying from an established vendor”. Trimble, AVEVA, Autodesk and Nomitech are established enterprises; FabTrol is the example of what happens when a niche vendor is absorbed and sunset.
  7. Thinner content library for MEP. For pure commercial HVAC/plumbing/duct, the manufacturer/price content of QuoteSoft, AutoBid, ESTmep and FastPIPE/FastDUCT (TRA-SER, Harrison, 100k+ items) is deeper than Quotestimate's Line 02 SMACNA offering.

Bottom line

If the job is to bid code fabrication work across piping, vessels, exchangers, structural and tanks — especially sour service, PWHT/NDE-heavy, or shop-to-site structural — Quotestimate is the most complete and most transparent estimator on this list.

If the job is to run a structural steel plant, or to design vessels to code, pair it with — or choose instead — Tekla / STRUMIS for production control and COMPRESS for design. They are complements, not substitutes, for what Quotestimate does.
